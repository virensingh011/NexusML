Model Limitations

Independent variables assumed. In reality, climate vulnerability correlates with infrastructure quality and response capacity. Emergency response times below 3 minutes are theoretical. The model extrapolates with decreasing confidence beyond observed global data. Linear weighting simplifies complex interactions. Real disaster risk involves non-linear thresholds, cascading failures, and feedback loops. Drift simulation shifts centroids uniformly. Real drift is feature-specific and non-uniform.

For production: calibrate weights against historical data, add correlated feature constraints, update baselines annually, and retrain on drift detection.
